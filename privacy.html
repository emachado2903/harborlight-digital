Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Harborlight Digital (“we,” “us”) designs custom websites for small businesses in San Antonio and Austin, Texas. This policy explains what information this website, harborlightdigital.co, collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. We’ve kept it short and plain, because there isn’t much to it.

The short version

  • We only collect what you choose to send us through the contact form or by email.
  • We use it to reply to you and to work on your project.
  • We don’t sell your information, and this site doesn’t use analytics, ad trackers, or tracking cookies.
  • You can ask us to see, correct, or delete your information at any time.

Information we collect

Information you give us

When you fill out our contact form, we receive your name, email address, business name (if you include it), the package you’re interested in, and your message. If you email us directly, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message.

Information collected automatically

This site doesn’t use analytics, advertising trackers, or tracking cookies, and we don’t build profiles of visitors. Like most websites, our hosting provider keeps basic technical logs, such as IP addresses, to keep the site secure and running. We don’t use those logs to identify you.

Your light or dark setting

If you switch between light and dark mode, that choice is saved in your own browser so the site remembers it next time. It stays on your device and is never sent to us.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your inquiry and answer your questions
  • To prepare a quote and, if you become a client, to plan, build, and support your website
  • To keep a record of our conversations and agreements

We won’t add you to a mailing list or send you marketing emails unless you ask us to.

Who we share it with

We don’t sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share it only with the services that help us run the business, and only so they can do that job:

  • Formspree, which delivers contact form messages to our inbox
  • Zoho Mail, which hosts our business email
  • GitHub Pages, which hosts this website

Each of these companies has its own privacy policy. We may also share information if the law requires it, or if it’s needed to protect our rights or someone’s safety.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry messages only as long as we need them to respond and for reasonable business records. If you become a client, we keep project information for as long as we work together and as long as we need it for our records afterward. You can ask us to delete your information sooner at any time.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you the personal information we have about you, correct it, or delete it. Email hello@harborlightdigital.co and we’ll respond within 30 days. We may need to keep some details when the law requires it, such as records of payments.

Do Not Track

Some browsers offer a “Do Not Track” setting. This site doesn’t track visitors across other websites, and we don’t allow other companies to collect information about your activity here for that purpose, so the site works the same whether or not the setting is on.

Keeping your information safe

This site is served over a secure, encrypted connection, and access to our accounts is protected. No way of sending or storing information online is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect what you share with us.

Children’s privacy

This site is meant for business owners and isn’t directed to children under 13. We don’t knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we’ll delete it.

Links to other websites

This site may link to other websites, such as sites we’ve built for clients. We aren’t responsible for how those sites handle your information, so we encourage you to read their privacy policies.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we’ll post the new version on this page and change the “Last updated” date at the top.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your information? Email hello@harborlightdigital.co.
